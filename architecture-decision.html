Architecture Decision

Module: release-readiness-uat-governance-pack

Decision: Build a narrow readiness-governance module focused on UAT and launch evidence before human release decisions.

1. Operating problem

Releases often slip or create rework when UAT starts before scope, test evidence, environments, data, SMEs, defects, and signoff ownership are ready. Leaders need a practical gate that turns readiness concerns into visible evidence, blockers, decisions, and launch conditions.

2. Existing adjacent modules

Adjacent moduleExisting ownership

Project Charter Initiation AgentAuthorized scope, objectives, governance, ownership, assumptions, risks, and planning handoff.

PMO Governance Operations LogGovernance notes, blockers, actions, decisions, escalations, and follow-through.
Value Realization Governance LedgerBenefits tracking, baselines, targets, actuals, evidence gaps, and continue/stop/scale review.

3. What is missing

The portfolio needs a readiness gate between charter/planning and launch/executive/governance follow-through. The missing layer is not another status report; it is a structured review of whether business validation or launch can responsibly proceed based on evidence, environment/data readiness, test coverage, defects, signoff ownership, and unresolved risks.

4. Lifecycle position

This module sits after chartering, planning, and sequencing, and before launch approval, executive review, governance closeout, and post-launch value tracking.

5. What this module owns

  • UAT and launch readiness intake.
  • Entry and exit criteria framing.
  • Evidence readiness review.
  • Environment and data readiness review.
  • Test coverage and defect/blocker visibility.
  • Signoff matrix and ownership gaps.
  • Launch decision brief for human review.
  • Handoff notes to governance, executive review, and value realization modules.

6. What adjacent modules own

  • Business Case System owns investment logic.
  • Project Charter Initiation Agent owns authorization and planning handoff.
  • Portfolio Prioritization Scoring Agent owns scoring and sequencing support.
  • Executive Portfolio Review Pack Builder owns executive decision forum packaging.
  • PMO Governance Operations Log owns ongoing follow-through and action tracking.
  • Value Realization Governance Ledger owns post-launch outcome and benefits tracking.

7. Accepted inputs

Release scope, milestone dates, UAT plan excerpts, acceptance criteria, test coverage notes, defect summaries, environment status, data readiness notes, workstream updates, SME availability, dependency lists, signoff owner lists, launch constraints, and previous charter/planning handoff notes.

8. Produced outputs

Readiness pack, gate assessment, readiness scorecard, entry/exit criteria, blocker list, evidence gaps, environment/data readiness screen, signoff matrix, escalation list, launch decision brief, and downstream handoff summary.

9. Downstream handoffs

OutputDownstream destination
Launch decision request, launch risk, no-go rationale, conditional launch tradeoffsExecutive Portfolio Review Pack Builder
Blockers, owners, due dates, unresolved decisions, escalation pathsPMO Governance Operations Log
Post-launch assumptions, value risks, baseline/target items, measurement triggersValue Realization Governance Ledger

10. Human-owned decisions

Humans retain authority for UAT entry, UAT exit, launch approval, release delay, defect acceptance, control waiver, risk acceptance, funding, scope change, support readiness, finance/accounting validation, security/privacy/compliance approval, and executive communication.

11. Non-overlap boundary

This module assesses readiness for validation or launch. It does not justify the project, authorize the project, prioritize the portfolio, run testing, manage defects, execute deployment, replace quality owners, or track realized benefits after launch.
