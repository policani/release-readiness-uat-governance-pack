# Launch Decision Brief Template

## Brief structure

### 1. Decision required

State the exact decision needed:

- Enter UAT
- Hold UAT entry
- Exit UAT
- Launch
- Launch with conditions
- Hold launch
- No-go
- Defer decision pending evidence

### 2. Recommendation for human review

Use one of:

- Proceed
- Proceed with conditions
- Hold
- No-go
- Insufficient evidence to recommend

Do not present the recommendation as approval.

### 3. Readiness summary

| Dimension | Score | Status | Evidence | Concern |
|---|---:|---|---|---|

### 4. Blockers and material risks

| Item | Severity | Impact | Owner | Due date | Decision needed |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

### 5. Signoff view

| Signoff area | Owner | Status | Decision still required |
|---|---|---|---|

### 6. Launch conditions if proceeding

List conditions that must be met before launch. Include owner, evidence, and deadline.

### 7. Risks of proceeding

State plausible consequences based on evidence. Do not exaggerate.

### 8. Risks of holding

State operational, financial, stakeholder, sequencing, or commitment impacts if launch is held.

### 9. Human decisions required

List each decision, decision owner, options, timing, and unresolved assumptions.

### 10. Downstream handoffs

Identify which items go to Executive Portfolio Review Pack Builder, PMO Governance Operations Log, and Value Realization Governance Ledger.

## Quality rule

The brief should make the decision inspectable in five minutes by a time-constrained sponsor.
