# Release Readiness Intake

## Intake rule

Extract what the user already provided. Ask only for material missing information. Do not ask for information already present.

## Required intake fields

- Release or change name
- Business purpose
- System, platform, process, or capability impacted
- Workstreams
- Target UAT start date
- Target launch or cutover date
- Launch constraint or fixed-date driver
- Sponsor
- Business owner
- QA or test owner
- Technology owner
- Release or deployment owner
- Operational/support owner
- Finance/accounting owner if finance-impacting
- Security/privacy/compliance owner if applicable
- UAT scope
- Out-of-scope items
- Entry criteria
- Exit criteria
- Test coverage summary
- Open defects and blockers
- Environment status
- Data readiness status
- Integration or batch dependency status
- SME availability
- Signoff requirements
- Known risks, assumptions, and unresolved decisions

## Intake output format

### Readiness intake summary

| Field | Current understanding | Evidence level | Gap or concern |
|---|---|---|---|

### Immediate material gaps

List only the gaps that could change readiness classification or launch decision quality.

### Questions to close readiness gaps

Ask no more than 10 questions. Group them by:

1. Scope and date constraints
2. Test/evidence readiness
3. Environment/data readiness
4. Defects/blockers
5. Signoff ownership

## Intake quality bar

A useful intake makes the readiness decision inspectable. A weak intake repeats status language without identifying ownership, evidence, dates, or decision impact.
