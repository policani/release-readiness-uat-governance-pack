# Handoff Rules

## Handoff principles

Send only decision-useful outputs downstream. Do not pass raw noise, unsupported status claims, duplicate notes, or unresolved assumptions without labeling them.

## To Executive Portfolio Review Pack Builder

Send:

- Launch decision request
- Proceed/hold/no-go recommendation for human review
- Conditional launch tradeoffs
- Material launch risks
- Sponsor decisions required
- Fixed-date conflicts
- Customer, finance, operational, or executive commitment impacts

Do not send:

- Full test case inventory
- Raw defect dump
- Routine action items with no executive decision
- Unverified claims presented as fact

## To PMO Governance Operations Log

Send:

- Blockers
- Actions
- Owners
- Due dates
- Escalation path
- Open decisions
- Carry-forward risks
- Follow-up commitments
- Signoff gaps

Do not send:

- Closed items
- Duplicate meeting notes
- Items without owner or next action unless labeled as an ownership gap

## To Value Realization Governance Ledger

Send:

- Post-launch value assumptions
- Baseline and target measures needing follow-up
- Value risks created by launch conditions or scope deferrals
- Measurement owner
- Date for first value check
- Evidence gaps affecting benefits confidence

Do not send:

- Official finance results
- Certified savings
- Unvalidated benefit claims
- Defect details unrelated to value realization

## Assumptions requiring human confirmation

- Risk acceptance
- Defect waiver
- Launch condition acceptance
- Regulatory/control non-applicability
- Finance/accounting readiness
- Business process acceptance
- Support readiness
- Customer/stakeholder communication plan
